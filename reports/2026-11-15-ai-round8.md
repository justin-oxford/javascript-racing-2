- **Wake:** one path trail now gives both tow and dirty air (WAKE = ALONG × SIDEWAYS × SPEED RATIO). It is ON, follows curves, and has no dead zone close behind. The old wake is kept behind `slipstream.trail.enabled` for comparison only.

- **Dirty-air max ships at 10%,** your default. `ai.brain` stays "rules". 189 tests pass.
