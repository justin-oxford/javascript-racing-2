# AI round 7: following at Oxford scale (2026-11-15)

The rescale is `ai.follow` in `config/ai.json`. It is off by default, and with it off the AI behaves exactly as before. Turn it on with `--set ai.follow.enabled=1`. Run: `npm run race-realism -- --brain rules,lookahead`.

Seeds are the same as round 6. "Old" = switch off; "rescaled" = `ai.follow.enabled=1`. The duel tests (4–6) were re-run today with the new tool. Every other "old" column is round 6's run: with the switch off the code path is the same, and a unit test checks this.

## 1. Summary

- **The rescale works on the gap.** Gaps are now in car lengths (2.9–5 m, was 7–25 m) and 0.75 m into braking zones. Each car brakes at its own point, goes alongside with 0.3 m clearance, and AGGRESSION closes the gaps. It is built into both brains.
- **Gap at 2 s a lap:** the closest point through the braking zone is now 19 → 10 m (rules) and 17 → 7 m (look-ahead), and the follower reaches the gearbox (minimum about 0 m).
- **It doesn't buy passes:** at 2 s a lap, 8% → 4% (rules) and 9% → 5% (look-ahead). Packs get rougher: heavy hits per 20 laps 0.0 → 2.0 and 0.5 → 3.0, and stuck events 0.6 → 5.0 and 1.4 → 8.0.
- **The real stall is dirty-air tyre wear:** up to 26× within 30 m, so a close follower gets slower lap by lap. With the wear multiplier at 5 (probe only, not shipped), the gap to the car ahead in the braking zone (closest point) drops to 3–4 m and passes rise to 17–23%. Indiana's reverse grid passes (3rd–4th), and test 5's attacker is within 1 s on 90% of laps.
- **Recommendation:** leave `ai.follow` off for now. Decide dirty-air wear first, because it affects players too. Then re-test the rescale with lower wear.
