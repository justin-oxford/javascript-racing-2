## 1. Summary

1. **The new Oxford is live as layout version 2.** Old PBs and ghosts are kept apart, and old ghost files are refused with a message. Racing line, EXIT line and base setup are rebuilt; S1 and S2 now reach the new road edges.
2. **Wall hits are down about three-quarters:** rules 41 → 9 and look-ahead 45 → 12 per 100 car-laps. The worst v1 spot (the top-left exit onto the main straight) went from 508 hits to 36.
3. **Car-to-car heavy hits are up:** all races, rules 50 → 71 and look-ahead 72 → 90 (in the same 48 races). The PASS / MISS counts barely move (rules 20/13 → 18/15, look-ahead 25/8 → 22/11), and that is about the noise of regenerating alone.
4. **One spot breaks on the new layout:** the middle-right hairpin. Racing line point 101 sits on the grass tip of the island, and cars get stuck there (line points 95–102).
5. **No AI or car values changed, and the art is untouched.** Three tests were tied to v1 and are now layout-independent.
