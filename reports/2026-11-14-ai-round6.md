# AI round 6: race realism suite and look-ahead fixes (2026-11-14)

All runs are on the same seeds. Run it with `npm run race-realism -- --brain rules,lookahead`.
- Oxford: 20 seeds per short test. The duels (tests 4–6) have 40 seeds, and a race counts only when the attacker is still behind after lap 1.
- Indiana: 10 seeds for the reverse grid and the start.
- The long race has 8 seeds.

"Look-ahead final" is the look-ahead brain with parts A, B and D on. Part C is in the code but off.

## 1. Summary

- **What was built:** `npm run race-realism`, which runs 11 tests on both brains (329 runs in parallel, about 30 min). Part A fixes the look-ahead planner, part B adds look-behind defending, part C a crossover option (off), part D a start mode. Every change has its own `ai.lookahead` key.
- **What worked:**
  - With all four parts on, Oxford races had fewer stuck events (61 without part A → 40) and fewer heavy hits (29 without part B → 19).
  - Starts went 3-wide into turn 1 in 5 of 20 starts (was 0), with fewer lap-1 heavy hits (3 → 1).
- **What didn't:**
  - Neither brain meets the main targets. A faster car still can't get through: from last on the grid the fastest car finishes 8th–9th, and the long race doesn't end in pace order.
  - In the long races the final look-ahead had more stuck events than the baseline look-ahead (60 → 96), its back-marker loss rose to 1.16 s, and part C never fired.
- **Recommendation:** keep `ai.brain = "rules"`. Rules is still the cleanest on heavy hits, stuck events and back markers, and costs half as much. Neither brain is more realistic yet.

| Part | What it does (key in `ai.lookahead`) | Result |
|---|---|---|
| A: predict every nearby car | Predicts cars up to 15 m behind as well as ahead, and how each one is drifting sideways (`behindM`, `latDecay`). | Fewer stuck events (61 → 40) and heavy hits (22 → 19). |
| A: after spins | A car that is slow or pointing well off the line is predicted to recover slowly and given more room (`hazardAccel`, `hazardWidthM`). The car's own planner waits until it has been pointing along the line and moving for 1 s (`settleSec`, `settleHeadingRad`). | Stuck after a spin: 29 → 14. |
| A: safety beats hysteresis | The current choice keeps its hold bonus only while its run is predicted clean (`holdSafeCostM`). Re-planning and reaction scale with RACECRAFT (`craftReact`). | Unit test: a car that drifts into the chosen lane makes the planner drop it. |
| A: ATTACK split from the close-up | Within 1 s of the car ahead, the attacker stays in ATTACK (margins and deploy), but that never starts the close-up (`attackStayGapSec`). | The close-up is unchanged. |
| B: look-behind | Into each braking zone, the defender predicts the attacker to the braking point and covers only if holding would likely lose the place and covering costs less (`defend`). It never touches braking and makes one decision per zone. | Heavy hits 29 → 19 in races. 0 straights with 2+ moves. Defenders cover rarely: 8 moves in 200 duels. Covers with nobody within 1.5 s: 5 of those 8, a MISS. |
| C: crossovers | Behind a car covering the inside before a 100 m+ straight, the planner is credited for an outside entry by the exit speed it gains (`cutback`). | **Never fired:** results identical with and without it. Oxford has one such corner, and with part B defenders rarely cover. Left off. |
| D: start mode | Seeded launches from CONSISTENCY: good, wheelspin or bogging. From lights out the planner tries lanes across the whole road and holds its lane when there's nothing to plan around. AGGRESSION brakes later into turn 1. It hands over to the rules near the first braking zone (`start`). | 3-wide into turn 1: 0 → 5 of 20 starts. Lap-1 heavy hits 2 → 1, stuck 4 → 2. Lap-1 taps 108 → 178. Aggression still doesn't buy places (−0.04). |

The long-race stuck rise (60 → 96) is mostly stuck events after a wide mistake (17 → 45). It doesn't come from any one part: removing A, B or D alone still leaves 87–101. With every round-6 key off, the code reproduces the baseline exactly, so it is how the parts combine.

## 4. Decisions I made on my own

| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| `ai.brain` stays "rules" | Your call, and the numbers don't argue for a change. |
| Every round-6 change has its own `ai.lookahead` key; removing the key restores round 5 exactly | Verified on 3 races per brain: identical results. |
| Start mode and look-behind apply to the look-ahead brain only | The rules baseline stays untouched and comparable. |

| Suite design: grid as the card says; the ladder's slower car is the same car with its cornering and braking margins scaled down (bisected to 0.2 / 0.5 / 1 / 2 s a lap); duels where the attacker leads after lap 1 don't count | Like-for-like, deterministic duels. |
| Suite fixes after the baseline: the ladder could only reach 1.7 s (it now reaches 2.0), duel seeds doubled to 40, the stuck-cause label fixed | Both brains' duel baselines were re-run with the fixed tool. |
